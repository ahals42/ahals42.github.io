SMARTBot

LLM chatbot designed for self-monitoring and SMART goal setting to promote physical activity guideline adherence in insufficiently active adults. Development focuses on the utilisation of persistent memory profiles and transcripts, as well as wearable active minutes, to tailor the experience and provide enhanced personalisation. Evaluated with internal usability testing and forthcoming focus group interviews.

Reframing Retirement Coach

LLM chatbot designed to serve as a supplementary tool for participants in the University of Victoria Behavioural Medicine Lab's forthcoming Reframing Retirement feasibility study and RCT. Development focuses on the utilisation of a RAG-grounded knowledge base derived from an existing validated physical activity behaviour change intervention. Evaluated with automated RAG evaluation for retrieval quality and semantic overlap with a curated knowledge base, as well as human expert ratings of chatbot responses for theoretical alignment, safety and scope adherence, content accuracy, delivery quality, personalisation, and population appropriateness.
