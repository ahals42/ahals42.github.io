HRV as a Moderator of Adiposity and Aerobic Fitness

Examined whether resting HRV (RMSSD) moderates the relationship between body fat percentage and aerobic fitness (VO₂peak) in overweight adults using hierarchical regression. Higher HRV appeared to buffer the negative effect of adiposity on fitness, though the interaction fell short of conventional significance.
