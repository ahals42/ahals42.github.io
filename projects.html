HRV as a Moderator of Adiposity and Aerobic Fitness Analysis

Investigated whether autonomic flexibility buffers the negative relationship between body fat percentage and aerobic fitness in 35 overweight adults using hierarchical regression and Johnson-Neyman analysis. Higher RMSSD may attenuate the negative effect of adiposity on VO₂peak, with the relationship becoming nonsignificant at high HRV levels.
